## CORE CONCEPT

ONE LINE. THREE TRANSFORMATIONS. COUNTLESS LIVES.

It descends underground, revealing geological layers and a groundwater aquifer.

It becomes the drilling mechanism, the well casing, the pump, and finally flowing water.

The droplet becomes the visual symbol of a life changed.

The line must NEVER disappear. Every new scene must be constructed from the geometry, lines, shapes, and typography of the previous scene.
